Status: needs-triage
Blocked by: 04
Spec: [spec.md](../spec.md) · Design: [tech-design.md](../tech-design.md)

## Summary

Members ask and answer questions on the Class page, before, during and after the Class.

## Scope

- `qa_posts` table (questions and replies).
- Post a question; reply to a question. Any Member can do both.
- Mark replies whose author is an Instructor in this Community (computed at read time).
- Authors can edit their own posts; Instructors, Class coordinators and the Community admin can delete any post.
- Posts by deleted Accounts show as "deleted user".
- Refresh during a Live Class (polling every few seconds is enough for v1).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Any Member can post a question and reply to one, in any Class state.
- [ ] An Instructor's reply is visibly marked.
- [ ] An author can edit their own post and the post shows as edited; they can't edit others' posts.
- [ ] A Class coordinator can delete any post.
- [ ] After an Account is deleted, its posts show "deleted user".

## Out of scope

- The email when an Instructor answers (issue 10).
